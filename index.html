About TBARC Hiking

Connecting People with Nature, One Trail at a Time

TBARC Hiking is an initiative of the Tropical Biodiversity Action and Research Centre (TBARC) that encourages people to explore the natural world through hiking, nature walks and outdoor learning experiences.

Every trail offers an opportunity to discover the beauty of our natural landscapes, observe fascinating plant and animal life, and understand the ecological importance of the places we visit. From forest paths and mountain slopes to streams and countryside trails, hiking helps us build a deeper connection with nature.

Through guided hikes, biodiversity observation, nature interpretation and community activities, TBARC Hiking aims to combine outdoor recreation with environmental awareness and conservation.

Explore With Us

What We Do

Our activities connect outdoor exploration with biodiversity discovery, ecological learning and responsible adventure.

Activity 01

Nature Hikes

Explore scenic trails and natural landscapes while developing a deeper appreciation for the environment. Each hike provides an opportunity to experience the landscape and understand its natural features.

Activity 02

Biodiversity Walks

Discover local flora and fauna through observation, nature photography and interpretation. Learn to recognise the diverse forms of life that often go unnoticed along familiar trails.

Activity 03

Educational Expeditions

Create opportunities for students and nature enthusiasts to learn about ecosystems, ecological interactions, habitats and conservation through guided outdoor experiences.

Activity 04

Responsible Adventure

Promote safe, inclusive and environmentally responsible hiking practices. Encourage participants to follow trail rules, respect natural habitats and minimise their environmental footprint.

Activity 05

Community Activities

Bring people together through outdoor experiences, environmental awareness programmes and collaborative initiatives that encourage a shared responsibility towards nature.

Activity 06

Nature Observation and Photography

Encourage participants to document landscapes, plants, wildlife and ecological details through photography and observation, always prioritising the well-being of wildlife and habitats.

Our Philosophy

The Trail Is a Classroom

A hike is more than a journey through a landscape. It is an opportunity to learn from nature, develop curiosity and discover the connections that sustain life around us.

Every Step Can Teach Us Something

A tree, a butterfly, a stream or a patch of moss can reveal fascinating stories about ecological relationships and the natural world. Paying attention to these details helps us recognise the value of biodiversity in everyday landscapes.

At TBARC Hiking, we believe that meaningful experiences in nature can inspire curiosity, encourage responsible behaviour and strengthen our commitment to biodiversity conservation.

Our aim is not simply to reach a destination, but to understand, appreciate and protect the natural places we experience along the way.

Our Trail Ethics

Our Commitment to Responsible Hiking

Exploring nature comes with a responsibility to protect it. We encourage every participant to follow these principles during hiking and outdoor activities.

♻️

Carry Back Your Waste

Take all waste back with you and avoid single-use plastics wherever possible.

🦌

Respect Wildlife

Observe animals from a safe distance. Never chase, feed, touch or deliberately disturb wildlife.

🥾

Stay on Permitted Trails

Follow designated routes, protected-area rules and the instructions of hike coordinators.

🌱

Leave Nature Undisturbed

Avoid collecting plants, animals, rocks or other natural materials. Leave them where they belong.

🔇

Keep Noise to a Minimum

Respect the tranquillity of natural habitats and avoid loud music or unnecessary disturbance.

🧭

Prioritise Safety

Follow safety instructions, weather advisories and coordinator guidance. Carry suitable equipment and remain with your group.

🤝

Respect Local Communities

Be considerate of local cultures, traditions, livelihoods, private property and community spaces.

🌿

Protect Habitat Integrity

Avoid trampling vegetation, damaging trails or spreading seeds and plant material between habitats.

Leave Only Footprints. Take Only Memories.

Explore responsibly. Learn continuously. Protect every trail.
